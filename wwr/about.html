Our Story

Wild River Rafting was founded in 1995 by a group of passionate outdoor enthusiasts who believed everyone deserves to experience the thrill of white-water rafting. For over 25 years, we have been guiding adventurers through some of the most breathtaking rivers in the country.

What started as a small family business with two rafts has grown into a full-service adventure company offering trips for all skill levels — from calm family floats to heart-pounding class V rapids.
